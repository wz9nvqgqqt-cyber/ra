      Frequently asked questions

      Common questions about steel buildings.

      Every project is unique. These answers help align expectations before engaging an engineer or fabricator.

      How accurate is the estimator?

      The estimator is designed for early budgeting. It uses typical industry ranges and configurable multipliers. Always confirm with a structural engineer and steel supplier for final pricing.

      Does it include foundations or site work?

      The default budget range covers the building structure and envelope. Site work, foundations, and interior fit-out are noted separately in the scope checklist.

      Can I reuse the output in a proposal?

      Yes. Copy the summary values and checklist to share internally or with clients as a preliminary planning document.
